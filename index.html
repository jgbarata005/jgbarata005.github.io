About

I'm a junior at Caltech studying electrical engineering on the Intelligent Systems track. I like projects that cross the whole stack, from laying out a 48 V motor board to tuning the controller that keeps a robot upright.

Right now I'm a research intern in Caltech's Autonomous Robotics and Control Lab, bringing reinforcement learning policies onto a Booster T2 humanoid. Last fall I was the head Altium TA for EE13, mentoring more than 30 students through PCB design and hardware debugging.
