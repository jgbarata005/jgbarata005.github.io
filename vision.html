Stereo Ball Tracker

Two synchronized cameras track a thrown ball in 3D, a Kalman filter predicts where it will land, and a projector marks that spot on the floor before the ball arrives.

Detection and calibration

Two global shutter cameras feed a Python and OpenCV pipeline that finds the ball with HSV color segmentation and contour detection. I calibrated each camera's intrinsics from checkerboard images and found their positions in the room with solvePnP. Detection was tuned to keep working under the projector's light.

3D tracking and prediction

Each 2D detection becomes a ray in world space, and a custom stereo triangulation finds where the two rays meet. A 6-state Kalman filter estimates the ball's 3D position and velocity, and predicts where it will hit the floor under gravity.

Projection and hardware

The predicted impact point is converted to projector floor coordinates and sent over TCP and WiFi to a second computer, which draws the landing spot on the floor before the ball gets there. I designed custom mounts for the two cameras and the projector.

Drawing mode

I also added an interactive drawing mode. Instead of predicting a landing spot, the projector draws on the floor wherever the tracked ball goes, so moving the ball draws a picture.
